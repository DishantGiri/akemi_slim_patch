How to Use Akemi Slim Patch

For best use, apply Akemi Slim Patch to clean, dry skin. Press firmly so the patch sticks well. Avoid applying it over irritated skin, cuts, lotion, or heavy sweat.

01

Clean and dry the skin

Wash your target area gently and dry thoroughly before application to ensure maximum adhesion.

02

Carefully separate the adhesive patch from its backing paper, keeping the sticky side clean.

03

Apply the patch firmly

Press the patch onto your arm or chosen area for 10-15 seconds to ensure secure grip.

04

Wear as directed

Let the patch transfer ingredients comfortably throughout your day, fully hidden under clothing.

05

Replace with a new patch daily

Remove the used patch daily and apply a fresh one to a slightly different skin location.

06

Use consistently

Application Tips for Best Results

Consistency matters more than intensity when using wellness-support products like Akemi Slim Patch.

Best Practices

Apply to clean, dry skin

Wash and dry your chosen skin surface completely before application to guarantee optimal transdermal stick.

Avoid oily or lotion-covered areas

Lotions, skin oils, and heavy creams weaken the medical-grade adhesive, leading to lifting or sliding.

Rotate spots regularly

Avoid applying the patch to the exact same spot daily. Rotate placement to let each area breathe and stay clean.

Use consistently as directed

Establish a solid routine by applying a new patch at the same time each day, making wellness completely automatic.

Stay hydrated daily

Drink plenty of water throughout the day to support normal metabolism, internal balance, and cellular energy.

Balanced nutrition & movement

Pair your daily patch with wholesome, balanced meals and simple physical activity to reinforce real results.
